## Editorial rules (keep the site confident, not needy)
- **Evidence over adjectives.** A claim earns its place only if a visitor can open something that backs it. No self-awarded badges ("Verified"), no tool-logo walls, no skills the work above doesn't show.
- **One ask per moment.** Header CTA, hero CTA, pricing cards, case-study footer, contact. No sticky bars, no repeated "work with me" bands, no marketplace links (Fiverr) beside premium pricing.
- **Say each fact once.** Projects appear in the work list and client block — not again as a timeline. Stats appear in the hero card — not again in a stats grid.
- **Show real activity only.** The GitHub contribution graph was removed because it undersold the work (private repos don't show). Curated repos with real descriptions beat a full list.
- **Don't promise what isn't shown.** No ad-management or traffic claims until there's a case study for it.
- **Label illustrations.** Concept art always says "Concept" until it's replaced by a real screenshot.
